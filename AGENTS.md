# Scholarly writing instructions

When editing this research project, follow the writing preferences in
[paper/WRITING_GUIDELINES_EL.md](paper/WRITING_GUIDELINES_EL.md).

Use concise, natural academic English and connected paragraphs. Avoid promotional
claims, generic transitions and repeated statements of the same limitation.
Preserve every scientific qualification needed to interpret the evidence.

Group related material into a small number of substantial sections. Use a
subsection only when it develops a distinct topic with enough content. For the
current manuscript, retain six main sections unless its content or journal
requirements give a concrete reason to change that structure.

Avoid narrative dashes and unnecessary compounds containing hyphens. Disable
automatic word hyphenation in the manuscript. Preserve mathematical notation,
official names, identifiers, links and bibliographic records accurately.

Keep Panagiotis Vionis as the sole manuscript author unless the user explicitly
changes the author list. Preserve the correct authors of cited publications.
Changes to style and structure must preserve numerical results, formulae and
the distinction between controlled corruptions, local tests and live deployment.
